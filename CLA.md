# Contributor License Agreement

Thank you for contributing to komando. This agreement explains what
happens to your contribution. Please read it before you send a pull
request. You sign it once, by writing a comment on your first pull request
(see [How to sign](#how-to-sign)).

## In plain words

- **You keep the copyright** of what you wrote.
- You give the maintainer permission to use your contribution, including
  in the [commercially licensed](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md) version of komando.
  This is what keeps komando funded.
- **komando stays open source.** Every contribution will always also be
  available under the AGPL-3.0 or another open-source licence approved by
  the [Open Source Initiative](https://opensource.org/licenses).
- You confirm that you are allowed to give your contribution.

The full text below is what counts.

## How to sign

When you open your first pull request, a bot asks you to sign. Write this
comment on the pull request:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

The bot records your GitHub name and the date. You only sign once, and it
covers all your future contributions to komando.

Signing for a company? Email **sugandaletters@gmail.com** first.
