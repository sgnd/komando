## Do I need one?

| You want to... | AGPL (free) | Commercial license |
|---|---|---|
| Use komando yourself, at home or at work | ✅ | not needed |
| Use komando unchanged inside your company | ✅ | not needed |
| Change komando and share your changes under the AGPL | ✅ | not needed |
| Change komando and keep your changes private, then give it to others (customers, partners, the public) | ❌ | ✅ |
| Build komando, or part of it, into a closed-source product | ❌ | ✅ |
| Offer a changed komando as a hosted service without publishing your changes | ❌ | ✅ |
| Use komando where your company's policy does not allow AGPL software | ❌ | ✅ |

In short: **the AGPL asks that changes are shared back.** When you give
komando to other people, or let them use it over a network, you must also
give them the source code of your version, under the AGPL. A commercial
license removes that duty.

Not sure? Ask, we are happy to help. This page is a summary, the
[LICENSE](LICENSE) file is what counts.

## What a commercial license includes

- Use, change and ship komando without the AGPL's duties.
- Priority support and security notices, if you choose a support plan.
- Help from the people who build komando.
