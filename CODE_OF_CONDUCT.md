# Code of conduct

In short: be kind, respect each other, and help beginners.
These rules apply to everyone in this project.

## Our promise

We want komando to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their
experience, background or identity. Beginners are especially welcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent; ask before you judge.
- Give and accept feedback about code, not about people.
- Remember that English is not everyone's first language.

## Not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would see as unprofessional.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place where
you represent the project.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer at the address on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/sgnd). Reports are kept
private. The maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or ban people who
break these rules.
